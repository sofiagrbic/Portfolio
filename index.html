About Me

I’m Sofia—a business student, creative strategist, and lifelong storyteller who believes the best brands aren’t built through trends; they’re built through connection.

Whether I'm interviewing strangers on the street, creating content for a growing business, or launching one of my own projects, I'm most inspired by understanding people. I love discovering what makes someone stop scrolling, start listening, and genuinely care.

I'm currently studying Business at the University of Colorado Boulder with a focus on Entrepreneurship and Strategy. I care about creating work that's thoughtful, authentic, and leaves people feeling a little more connected than before.
