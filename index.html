About Me

I'm Sofia—a business student, creative strategist, and lifelong storyteller who believes the best brands aren't built through trends; they're built through connection.

Whether I'm interviewing strangers on the street, creating content for a growing business, or launching one of my own projects, I'm most inspired by understanding people. I love discovering what makes someone stop scrolling, start listening, and genuinely care.

I'm currently studying Business at the University of Colorado Boulder with a focus on Entrepreneurship and Strategy while working across social media, branding, and creative marketing. Along the way, I've led marketing initiatives for startups, collaborated with national brands, managed content strategy, and built businesses from the ground up.

Above all, I care about creating work that's thoughtful, authentic, and leaves people feeling a little more connected than before.
